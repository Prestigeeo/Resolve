# Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) — Consumer Code of Practice & Telecom Redress Procedures

## Statutory Authority
- **Enacting Body:** Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC)
- **Legal Foundation:** Nigerian Communications Act 2003 (NCA 2003, Part VI, Sections 104–106); NCC Consumer Code of Practice Regulations 2007 (Article 126).
- **Public Escalation Channels:**
  - Toll-Free Redress Hotline: **622** (accessible from all Nigerian telecom networks)
  - Dedicated Portal: https://consumer.ncc.gov.ng | Email: consumer@ncc.gov.ng

---

## Section 1: Airtime and Data Purchase Failures (VTU vs. Direct Telco)
1. **Bank VTU Purchase (Aggregator Model):**
   - When a consumer purchases airtime or mobile data through their commercial bank mobile app or USSD channel, the transaction involves three parties: the Bank, a Value-Added Service (VAS) VTU aggregator, and the Mobile Network Operator (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile).
   - If the bank debits the user's account but the aggregator fails to hand the instruction over to the telco, the fault lies with the bank / aggregator.
   - **Resolution Rule:** If dialling `*310#` shows the balance was not credited, the consumer must first verify with the telecom provider whether a top-up packet was received. If not, the dispute must be lodged with the sender bank for instant aggregator reversal within **72 hours**.
2. **Direct Telco Purchase:**
   - Where purchase occurs directly through telecom channels (e.g. telecom scratch card, telecom self-care app, or telecom USSD menu) and data/airtime fails to reflect:
   - Telecom operators have a statutory obligation under NCC Quality of Service standards to resolve unapplied value within **24 hours**.

---

## Section 2: Escalation to NCC via Article 126 and Toll-Free 622
1. **Stage 1 Requirement (Direct Provider Lodgment):**
   - Article 126 of the NCC General Consumer Code dictates that consumers MUST first submit their complaint directly to their telecom operator's customer care service (via 180/300/phone or email) and obtain an official **Telecom Trouble Ticket Reference ID**.
2. **Stage 2 Escalation Window:**
   - If the telecom provider fails to resolve the undelivered airtime/data or bill dispute within the provider's published Service Level Agreement (SLA)—typically **72 hours to 7 business days**—or if the response is unsatisfactory:
   - The consumer has the legal right to escalate the dispute directly to the NCC Consumer Affairs Bureau by dialling **622** (toll-free) or submitting a written petition on the NCC complaints portal.
3. **Information Required for NCC Escalation:**
   - Mobile Phone Number debited or affected.
   - Telecom Network Operator (MTN, Airtel, Globacom, 9mobile).
   - Initial Complaint Ticket Number provided by the operator.
   - Date and time the problem occurred and amount debited.
   - Evidence of debit (Bank debit SMS/receipt or telecom top-up confirmation).
