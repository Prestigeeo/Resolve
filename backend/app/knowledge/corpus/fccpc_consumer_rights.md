# Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) — Commercial Non-Delivery & Consumer Redress

## Statutory Authority
- **Enacting Body:** Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC)
- **Legal Foundation:** Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 (FCCPA 2018), Sections 114–130.
- **Complaint Submission Portal:** https://complaints.fccpc.gov.ng | Email: contact@fccpc.gov.ng

---

## Section 1: Consumer Rights Concerning Timely Delivery of Goods and Services
1. **Section 114 (Consumer Right to Timely Performance):**
   - Every consumer in Nigeria has a legal right to the timely performance and completion of services, and the delivery of goods as explicitly promised by the vendor or supplier.
2. **Section 120 (Right to Goods Matching Description):**
   - Goods delivered must match their marketed samples and descriptions. Defective, counterfeit, or substandard merchandise entitles the buyer to a full refund and compensation.
3. **Section 127 (Unfair, Unreasonable or Deceptive Business Practices):**
   - Any merchant or online seller (including social media vendors on Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, Jiji) who accepts consumer funds with no intention or capacity to deliver, or who deliberately misleads consumers regarding delivery timelines, commits an unfair trade practice prohibited under federal law.

---

## Section 2: Statutory Remedies for Merchant Non-Delivery
1. **Immediate Right to Full Refund:**
   - Where a vendor or merchant fails to deliver agreed goods or services on or before the agreed delivery date, or within a reasonable statutory period:
   - The consumer has the absolute right to cancel the commercial contract and demand an immediate, unconditional full refund of all amounts paid.
2. **Formal Notice of Demand for Refund (The 7-Day Pre-Action Notice):**
   - Prior to initiating formal administrative enforcement or court action, the consumer is advised to issue a formal written **Notice of Demand for Full Refund**.
   - The notice gives the defaulting vendor **7 calendar days** to refund all monies paid.
   - The notice must detail: date of payment, exact amount paid, proof of payment (bank transfer receipt or online checkout confirmation), description of agreed items, agreed delivery deadline, and proof that the merchant missed delivery or ceased communication.

---

## Section 3: Escalation to the FCCPC
1. **Jurisdiction:**
   - The FCCPC possesses statutory powers to investigate online vendors, issue binding administrative orders compelling restitution/refunds, seal defaulting business premises, and prosecute recalcitrant merchants under the FCCPA 2018.
2. **Required Dossier for FCCPC Lodgment:**
   - Vendor Name, Brand/Business Name, Instagram/TikTok handle, WhatsApp phone number, and physical business address (if available).
   - Recipient Bank Account details (Bank Name, Account Name, and Account Number used to receive buyer's funds).
   - Chat screenshots showing invoice, price negotiation, agreed delivery date, and subsequent default or blocking.
   - Proof of Payment (Bank transfer receipt showing 30-digit NIBSS Session ID or checkout payment reference).
   - Copy of the 7-day Demand Notice delivered to the vendor.
