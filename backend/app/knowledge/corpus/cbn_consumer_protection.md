# Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) — Consumer Protection Framework & Dispute Escalation Regulations

## Statutory Authority
- **Enacting Body:** Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Consumer Protection Department (CPD)
- **Legal Foundation:** Central Bank of Nigeria Act 2007 (Section 2d); Banks and Other Financial Institutions Act (BOFIA) 2020; CBN Consumer Protection Framework (CPF).
- **Primary Contact Channel for Escalation:** cpd@cbn.gov.ng | CCMS Portal: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/FinInc/FinLit/LodgeComplaint.html

---

## Section 1: Mandatory Complaint Turnaround Window (The 14-Day Rule)
1. **Initial Provider Lodgment (Stage 1):** Every commercial bank, microfinance bank (MFB), payment service bank (PSB), and mobile money operator in Nigeria is legally required to receive, log, and issue a unique complaint tracking number (Ticket ID) to any consumer reporting a financial dispute.
2. **Statutory Timeframe:** Under Section 3.2 of the CBN Consumer Protection Guidelines, financial institutions have a strict maximum window of **14 calendar days** to investigate and conclusively resolve consumer electronic payment disputes.
3. **Right to Direct Regulatory Escalation (Stage 2):** If the financial institution fails to resolve the complaint, fails to reverse debited funds within 14 calendar days, or issues an unsatisfactory final response, the consumer acquires the immediate statutory right to lodge an escalation directly with the Central Bank of Nigeria Consumer Protection Department.

---

## Section 2: Requirements for CBN Consumer Protection Department (CPD) Petitions
To submit a legally valid Stage-2 complaint to the CBN CPD via `cpd@cbn.gov.ng`, the consumer's submission dossier must contain:
1. **Proof of Stage-1 Lodgment:** The original tracking/ticket reference issued by the bank or evidence of written notice delivered to the bank at least 14 days prior.
2. **Transaction Specifics:** Date of transaction, exact monetary debit amount in Naira (NGN), sender account number, recipient account number, and recipient financial institution.
3. **Session ID Reference:** For interbank electronic transfers, the 30-digit NIBSS Instant Payments (NIP) Session ID generated by the sender bank's switch.
4. **Statement of Account:** An official statement excerpt or stamped debit confirmation proving the debit was not reversed.
5. **Specific Relief Sought:** A clear request for immediate reversal of the unauthorized/failed sum, plus statutory refund of any associated transaction fees or unlawful maintenance charges.

---

## Section 3: Regulatory Sanctions on Non-Compliant Financial Institutions
- Banks that fail to report, conceal, or exceed the 14-day dispute resolution SLA are subject to mandatory CBN administrative fines of ₦2,000,000 per violation, in addition to daily accrued penalty fees until the consumer is made whole.
- Where a bank wrongfully holds customer funds beyond statutory reconciliation limits, the CBN may order refund with interest at the prevailing Monetary Policy Rate (MPR).
